Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India

About

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was the 11th President of India and a renowned aerospace scientist. He played a vital role in India's civilian space program and military missile development.

Achievements

  • President of India (2002-2007)
  • Developed India's first indigenous satellite launch vehicle
  • Recipient of Bharat Ratna
